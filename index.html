About

One developer,
four codebases.

MezuTech is one person. I pick problems I have myself — reading scripture without losing the thread, studying without lying to yourself about the hours, reading four hundred applications for one role — and build the narrowest app that solves them.

All four are still in progress, and the pages say so. Nothing on this site claims a user count, a rating or a launch date it has not earned. When an app ships, its page will change.
