Sift AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated January 5, 2026

Welcome to Sift AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of both recruiters and candidates who use our platform.

1. Information we collect

Recruiters

  • Account data: name, email address, company name, and payment information (processed securely via Paystack/LemonSqueezy).
  • Job data: job descriptions, screening questions, and hiring criteria.

Candidates

  • Application data: name, email address, LinkedIn profile, and answers to screening questions submitted via Sift links.
  • Uploaded documents: resumes/CVs, if applicable.

2. How we use AI

Sift AI uses large language models such as OpenAI and Google Gemini to process candidate applications. Specifically:

  • Ranking and scoring: we send anonymised candidate answers to our AI partners to evaluate them against the recruiter’s job description.
  • Summarisation: we generate brief summaries of candidate profiles to assist recruiters.

We do not use candidate data to train public AI models.

3. Third-party processors

We share data with the following service providers in order to operate the service:

  • AI providers: OpenAI, Google — for grading logic.
  • Hosting: Railway/AWS — for server infrastructure.
  • Email: Resend — for transactional email.
  • Payments: Paystack — for payment processing.

4. Data retention

We retain candidate data for the duration of the hiring process. Recruiters may delete job posts and their associated candidate data at any time. Inactive data is archived automatically after 24 months unless requested otherwise.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Candidates who want their data deleted should contact the recruiter who posted the job, or contact us directly at hello@syftup.com.

6. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, email hello@syftup.com.
