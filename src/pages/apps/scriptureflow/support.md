ScriptureFlow is built by one person. Email
**[support@scriptureflow.app](mailto:support@scriptureflow.app)** and that
person answers — usually within a couple of days.

The app's **Settings → Help** opens a message with your app version and device
already filled in, which saves a round trip. Use it when you can.

## Sign-in

**The six-digit code did not arrive.** Check spam first. Codes expire after a
few minutes, so request a fresh one rather than reusing an old email.
Requesting several in quick succession can trip a rate limit — wait a minute
and try once more. If two attempts fail, write to us with the address you
used.

**I want to sign in on a second device.** Use the same email address or the
same Apple ID. Your notes appear there if you are a Pro subscriber; without
Pro, notes stay on the device that wrote them.

**I never signed up and I have lost my phone.** An anonymous account cannot be
recovered — it exists only on that device, which is also why it needs nothing
from you. Adding an email address is what makes an account portable.

## Subscriptions

**Restoring a purchase.** Settings → Account → Restore purchases, signed in to
the same Apple ID or Google account that bought it. Purchases follow the store
account, not the email address you gave us.

**I paid and Pro is not showing.** Restore purchases first. If it still does
not appear, email us with your store receipt and we will fix the entitlement
by hand.

**Cancelling.** In your store, not in the app — the routes are on the
[deletion page](/apps/scriptureflow/delete-account). Cancelling in the store is
all that is needed; nothing has to be sent to us.

**Refunds** are issued by Apple or Google, not by us. Apple:
reportaproblem.apple.com. Google: your Play Store order history.

## Reading and study

**A study sheet has an empty section.** That is intended. When there is nothing
honest to say under a lens, the app shows the gap rather than filling it with
prose. It is not a failed load.

**Study material looks wrong.** It may well be — it is drafted by a language
model. Tell us the verse and the lens and we will look at it. Check anything
that matters against the text itself.

**Search found nothing.** Exact phrasing is matched first, then partial. If a
phrase is a paraphrase rather than wording that appears in the text, assisted
search tries to find the reference for you. Half-remembered wording is worth
retyping in a few different ways.

**A verse looks different from the Bible I know.** Check which translation you
are on in Reading Preferences. The app carries four public-domain
translations, and they render some verses quite differently from each other and
from copyrighted modern translations.

**Narration.** Not in the current release. It is built, and it is not switched
on, because the audio has to be licensed for the translation on screen first.
Nothing in the app promises it in the meantime.

## Widget and streaks

**The home-screen widget is blank.** Pin a verse from inside the app first —
the widget shows the verse you chose, not a random one. On iOS, remove and
re-add the widget after the first pin if it stays empty.

**My streak reset.** The streak counts days you showed up. A missed day
resizes a reading plan rather than zeroing it, but a gap in days is a gap in
the streak — we would rather show you the real number.

## Reporting a bug

Send us: what you did, what happened, what you expected, your device and OS
version, and the app version from Settings. A screenshot is worth several
paragraphs. If it involves a specific verse, name it.
