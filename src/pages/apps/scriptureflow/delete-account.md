You can delete your ScriptureFlow account and everything attached to it. There
is no retention period to wait out, no form to justify yourself on, and nobody
will email you to ask why.

## The fastest way: in the app

1. Open **ScriptureFlow**.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm once, on the sheet that appears.

The confirmation exists because this cannot be undone — not because we are
trying to talk you out of it.

## If you no longer have the app

Email **[support@scriptureflow.app](mailto:support@scriptureflow.app)** from the
address on your account, with the subject **Delete my account**. We will delete
it and reply to confirm, normally within a few days and always within 30 days.

If you signed in with Apple using **Hide My Email**, send the request from that
relay address so we can match it to your account. If you never added an email
address at all, your account is anonymous and we have no way to identify it
from the outside — deleting the app removes your access to it, and the account
holds nothing that identifies you.

## What is deleted

Everything keyed to your account, deleted immediately and permanently:

- Your notes and highlights on every verse
- Saved verses and your pinned widget verse
- Reading history, streaks, chapter completions and plan progress
- Reading plans you created yourself
- Your preferences and display name
- Your email address or Apple sign-in link, and the account record itself

## What is not deleted, and why

- **Purchase records.** If you subscribed, the App Store or Google Play keeps a record of that transaction, and so does RevenueCat. We cannot delete a payment record held by a store, and we are required to be able to account for a transaction that happened. That record is the purchase, not your reading.
- **Anything saved outside the app.** A verse image you shared, or notes you exported, is on your own device or in someone else's hands, and is beyond our reach.

## Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription

These are handled by two different companies. If you have an active Pro
subscription, **cancel it in your store first**, or it will keep renewing:

- **iPhone or iPad** — Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ScriptureFlow → Cancel.
- **Android** — Play Store → profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → ScriptureFlow → Cancel.

Deleting the app on its own does not cancel a subscription either.

## Keeping your notes

There is no export button yet. If you want to keep what you have written,
open each note and use the share action to send it to yourself before you
delete. If you have a large journal you want out in one piece, email us and we
will send you a file with your own data in it.
