## 1. What ScriptureFlow is

A Bible reading and study app. It presents public-domain scripture one verse at
a time, links each word to its Greek or Hebrew root, generates study notes, and
keeps reading plans and private journal entries.

It is a study aid. It is not a church, not spiritual direction, and not a
substitute for one.

**Scripture is free, always.** The whole biblical text, every translation in

If you stop subscribing, your notes are not taken from you. They remain on the
device that holds them; what stops is the syncing.

## 6. What the generated study material is

Study sheets, plain-language paraphrases, plan labels and search assistance are
produced with the help of language models.

**Treat them as a study aid, not as authority.** They can be wrong. They may
misread a nuance, flatten a disputed passage, or state a scholarly reading as
though it were settled. Where a study sheet has nothing honest to say, the app
shows you an empty section rather than filling it — that is deliberate, and it
is the same honesty we are asking of you here: check anything that matters
against the text itself and against people you trust.

We are not liable for any decision — theological, personal, financial or
otherwise — made on the basis of generated material.
