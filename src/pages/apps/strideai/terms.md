## 1. What StrideAI is

A study planner. It keeps tasks against subjects you create, runs a focus timer,
logs the hours you actually studied, and provides an AI coach you can ask about
a topic, a plan or a deadline.

It is a planning and study aid. It is not a tutor, not a qualified academic
adviser, and not a guarantee of any result in any exam.

## 4. Credits

The AI coach is metered in credits, and this is how they work:

- **200 credits** when you create your account.
- **200 free credits** each week.
- **10 credits** for each rewarded ad you choose to watch, up to **five ads a day**.

Spending is **1 credit** per coach message, **2** when the coach creates a task
for you, and **3** for analysing a document.

Two things follow from that, and we would rather say them plainly:

- **Credits are not money.** You cannot buy them, they have no cash value, they are not refundable and not transferable, and they do not survive the deletion of your account.
- **The on-device coach costs no credits at all.** Running a model on your own hardware costs us nothing, so charging you for it would not be defensible. Credits exist because the cloud backend runs on a shared free-tier allowance — a limited resource that has to be rationed between everyone using the app.

We may change these amounts as the shared allowance changes. If a change reduces
what you get, we will say so in the app rather than quietly adjusting a number.

## 5. Ads

StrideAI shows **rewarded ads only** — the kind you deliberately open in exchange

placed between you and your own study data.

We do not control which ads Google serves. If an ad is broken, misleading, or
inappropriate, tell us and we will pass it on, but the ad inventory is Google's.

Watching an ad and not receiving credits because of a network failure is a
support matter, not a lost purchase — see [support](/apps/strideai/support).

## 6. The AI coach, and what it is worth

The coach runs on general-purpose language models, either on your device or via
OpenRouter. Language models are wrong sometimes, and confidently so.

- **Check anything that matters** — a formula, a date, a definition, a claim about your syllabus — against your actual course materials.
- **It is not a substitute** for your teacher, your lecturer, or your institution's own guidance.
- **Do not submit its output as your own work.** Whether AI assistance is permitted in your coursework is a matter between you and your institution, and their rules govern, not ours. Academic misconduct is your responsibility.
- **Do not rely on it** for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions.

We do not warrant that any answer is accurate, complete, or suitable for your
purpose.
