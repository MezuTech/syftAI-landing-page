StrideAI is built by one person. Email
**[hello@syftup.com](mailto:hello@syftup.com)** and that person answers —
usually within a couple of days.

Telling us your device, your OS version and what you tapped just before the
problem saves a round trip. A screenshot saves two.

## Sign-in

**I forgot my password.** Use the reset link on the sign-in screen. The email
arrives from our authentication provider — check spam if it does not appear
within a few minutes.

**I signed up with Google and now cannot get in.** Make sure you are picking the
same Google account you originally used. An account created with Google and an
account created with the same address and a password are two different accounts.

**I want to use StrideAI on a second device.** Sign in with the same credentials.
Your subjects, tasks, study logs and credits are on your account, so they follow
you.

## Credits and ads

**I watched an ad and got no credits.** This is almost always a dropped
connection at the moment the ad finished. Reopen the app while online — if the
reward still has not landed, write to us with the rough time and we will credit
it manually.

**No ad is available to watch.** Ad inventory is Google's and it genuinely runs
dry sometimes, especially on a new install or a region with thin fill. It
usually returns within a few hours. The five-a-day cap also applies, and it
resets on its own.

**I have run out of credits and need the coach now.** Switch to the on-device
model. It costs no credits at all, works offline, and does not depend on ad
inventory or a shared allowance. It is on the AI model screen.

**Where did my credits go?** Every grant and every spend is recorded as a
transaction, so a balance can always be explained. Ask and we will walk you
through it.

## The AI coach

**Cloud answers are slow or failing.** The cloud backend runs on OpenRouter's
free tier, which is shared and rate-limited. When it is busy, answers queue or
fail. The on-device model is unaffected by this.

**The model download is stuck.** It is a single large file — use Wi-Fi, keep the
app in the foreground, and make sure you have enough free storage. If it fails
part-way, retry from the AI model screen rather than reinstalling.

**On-device answers are worse than cloud ones.** They will be. A model small
enough to run on a phone is not the equal of a hosted one — that is the trade
you are making for privacy, offline use and no credit cost.

**The coach said something wrong.** It will do that. Check anything that matters
against your actual course materials, and see
[the terms](/apps/strideai/terms) for what the coach is and is not.

## Reminders and the timer

**Reminders are not arriving.** Check notifications are allowed for StrideAI in
your system settings. On Android, battery optimisation is the usual culprit —
exempting StrideAI generally fixes it.

**The timer stopped when I locked my phone.** Keep the app open during a session
where you can. Aggressive background limits vary by manufacturer and are largely
outside our control.

## Your data

**How do I delete my account?** See
[the deletion page](/apps/strideai/delete-account).

**What leaves my phone?** [The privacy policy](/apps/strideai/privacy) says
exactly what, and to whom. The short version: switch the coach to on-device and
your questions and notes stay on the device.

## Known limits

- There is no in-app account deletion yet — deletion is by email while that ships.
- There is no web version. iOS and Android only.
- There is no data export button yet.
- Sign in with Apple is not offered yet; email or Google for now.
