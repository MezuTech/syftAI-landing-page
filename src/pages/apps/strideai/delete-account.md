You can delete your StrideAI account and everything attached to it. There is no
retention period to wait out, no form to justify yourself on, and nobody will
email you to ask why.

## How to request it

Email **[hello@syftup.com](mailto:hello@syftup.com)** from the address on your
account, with the subject **Delete my account**. We will delete it and reply to
confirm, normally within a few days and always within 30 days.

If you signed up with **Google**, send the request from that same Google
address so we can match it to your account.

We will not ask you to explain yourself, and we will not offer you a discount to
stay.

> **An in-app delete button is coming.** Until it ships, email is the route, and
> it does exactly the same thing. We would rather say that plainly than pretend
> the button already exists.

## What gets deleted

Everything keyed to your account, in one cascade:

- Your **subjects**, and their names, icons and colours.
- Your **tasks** — titles, notes, due dates and completion history.
- Your **study logs** — every hour recorded against every subject.
- Your **focus sessions** — every Pomodoro session and its outcome.
- Your **credit balance and credit transactions**.
- Your **ad-watch counts**.
- Your **account record** — email address, and the Google identifier if you used it.

There is no soft-delete tombstone quietly holding your study history after this.

## What lives on your phone, and how to remove it

Some things were never on our server, so deleting the account does not touch
them. **Deleting the app removes all of them:**

- The offline cache of your subjects, tasks and logs.
- Your timer and reminder settings.
- Your **AI chat history**, which is stored on the device and never uploaded.
- The **on-device language model**, if you downloaded it. This is the large one — deleting the app reclaims that storage.

## What we cannot delete

- **Your Google account.** If you signed up with Google, we can delete our record of you, but your Google account is yours and unaffected.
- **Ad data held by Google.** Google's advertising identifier and whatever AdMob recorded are governed by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not ours. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings, and on iOS you can withdraw tracking permission at any time.
- **Email you have already sent us.** If you wrote to support, that correspondence sits in a mailbox. Say so in your request and we will delete it too.

## Credits

Credits are not money, cannot be bought, and have no cash value — see
[the terms](/apps/strideai/terms). Any balance is deleted with the account and
there is nothing to refund.

## If you only want to stop the data leaving your phone

You may not need to delete anything. Switching the AI coach to the **on-device**
model means your questions, notes and study data stay on the device — no
question is sent to any provider. [The privacy
policy](/apps/strideai/privacy) explains the difference in full.
