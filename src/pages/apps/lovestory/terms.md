## 2. What LoveStory is

An app that writes romance novels to your specification. You pick a trope, a
setting, a heat level and a length; the app produces an outline and then
chapters, either on a model running on your own phone or through a hosted model
in the cloud.

It is a fiction generator. Every character in it is invented, every event is
invented, and nothing it produces is a statement about a real person.

## 6. What the generated fiction is, and is not

Stories are written by language models. They can be repetitive, clichéd,
structurally odd, or occasionally wrong about the world. That is the state of
the art at this size, not a fault you have hit.

More importantly:

- **It is fiction, and it is not advice.** Nothing generated is relationship, medical, legal, psychological or financial guidance, however confidently a character states it.
- **Resemblance to real people is coincidental.** If you supply a premise naming a real person, what comes back is invented text about that name, and you are responsible for what you then do with it.
- **A model can produce something you did not ask for and do not want.** Filters are imperfect in both directions. If the app produces something that should not exist, tell us — the report goes to a person.

We are not liable for any decision — personal, romantic, financial or otherwise
— made on the basis of generated fiction.
