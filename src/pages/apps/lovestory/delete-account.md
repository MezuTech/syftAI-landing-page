You can have your LoveStory account and everything attached to it deleted. There
is no retention period to wait out, no form to justify yourself on, and nobody
will email you to ask why.

## Today, this is an email request

**There is not yet a delete button inside the app.** LoveStory is still in
development and has not been submitted to the app stores; the in-app route is
being built and will be there before release. Saying otherwise on this page
would be more convenient and less true.

Until it lands, email **[hello@syftup.com](mailto:hello@syftup.com)** from the
address on your account, with the subject **Delete my LoveStory account**. We
will delete it and reply to confirm, normally within a few days and always
within 30 days.

Send it from the account's own email address. It is the only thing that ties the
request to the account, and we would rather be awkward about that than delete
the wrong person's library.

## What is deleted

Everything keyed to your account, removed permanently:

- Every story and chapter you generated and saved
- Your library and favourites
- Reading progress and the place you left off
- Ratings you gave
- Your settings — font, mature-content toggle, notifications
- Your credit balance and usage record
- Bug reports you submitted
- Your profile — email address, display name, bio, avatar — and the account record itself

The database cascades from the account row, so there is no soft-delete
tombstone holding your stories after the fact.

## What is not deleted, and why

- **Models you downloaded.** They live on your phone, not with us. Delete them in the app before you go, or delete the app, which takes them with it. They are large, so this is worth doing deliberately rather than leaving several gigabytes behind.
- **Stories you exported or shared.** Anything you sent to yourself or to someone else is on a device we have no reach into.
- **Stories generated on-device and never saved.** These were never ours. They never left your phone in the first place.

## There is no subscription to cancel

LoveStory has no subscription, no in-app purchase and no payment of any kind, so
there is nothing to cancel before deleting and no billing relationship to wind
down. Credits are free and simply cease to exist with the account.

## Keeping your stories

There is no bulk export yet. If you want to keep what you have generated, open
each story and use the share action to send it to yourself before you ask for
deletion. If you have a large library you want out in one piece, say so in the
same email and we will send you a file with your own stories in it.
